THE TANK: logos
===============

For each venture there is a full logo (icon + name, 2:1) and an icon on its own (square):

  lancaster-turnpike-logo / -icon       The Turnpike (1792)
  slaters-mill-logo / -icon             Slater's Mill (1792)
  north-river-steamboat-logo / -icon    The Steamboat (1808)
  erie-canal-logo / -icon               The Erie Canal (1817)
  northern-cross-railroad-logo / -icon  The Northern Cross Railroad (1837)

  the-tank-badge          "The Tank" emblem on a navy badge
  the-tank-emblem-gold    the emblem alone in gold (transparent background)
  the-tank-emblem-white   the emblem alone in white, for dark backgrounds

  contact-sheet.png       everything at a glance

.png  transparent background, high resolution (logos 2400x1200, icons 1024x1024). Use these in
      Google Slides, Docs, Canva, PowerPoint, etc.
.svg  vector versions with the fonts built in, for resizing without blur. They open in any web
      browser and most design tools.

Regenerate after changing a logo (js/logos/*.js or js/tanklogo.js):  node tools/export_logos.mjs
